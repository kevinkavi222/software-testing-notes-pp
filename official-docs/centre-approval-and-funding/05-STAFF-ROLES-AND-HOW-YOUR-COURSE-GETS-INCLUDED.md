# Staff roles, cheap quals, and how your course gets included

## 1. Do we definitely need Tutor / Assessor / IQA packs?

Yes — for almost every awarding-body route (Focus Awards, NCFE, Pearson, BCS regulated approval, etc.).

Freelance staff are usually acceptable if you submit their CV, ID, certificates, CPD, and a freelance/service agreement.

---

## 2. Can one person be Tutor + Assessor + IQA?

**Not cleanly. Plan for at least two people.**

| Combination | Usually OK? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Same person = **Tutor + Assessor** | Yes | Focus Awards explicitly allows this if both quals/competence are held |
| Same person = **Assessor + IQA** on the same learners | No | Conflict of interest — IQA cannot quality-assure their own assessment decisions |
| One person holding all three titles on the org chart | Risky / often refused | Focus Awards: *“Please make sure you have a separate staff member who is an IQA, as approval may be denied if they have more than one role”* |

### Practical minimum for a small centre

1. **Person A (you or lead trainer):** Tutor + Assessor  
2. **Person B (freelance IQA):** Internal Quality Assurer  

Person B can be part-time / per cohort. Many small centres hire a freelance IQA by the day or by sampling batch.

Trainee assessors/IQAs are sometimes allowed if a qualified person **countersigns** — but you still need that second qualified person available.

## 3. Cheap online courses (indicative — check current price & what’s included)

You need **Ofqual-regulated** versions, not random CPD certificates.

### What to buy

| Role | Usual qualification | Typical online price band |
|---|---|---|
| Tutor | Level 3 Award in Education and Training (AET) — or L4 CET if more experienced | often ~£150–£400 |
| Assessor | Level 3 Certificate in Assessing Vocational Achievement (**CAVA**) — most flexible | often ~£240–£480 |
| IQA | Level 4 Award in Internal Quality Assurance of Assessment Processes and Practice | often ~£200–£450 |
| Bundle | CAVA + IQA combined | often ~£900–£1,100 |

Example providers currently advertising online options (compare carefully; prices change):

- Excelon Learning — CAVA advertised around **£244**  
- Teacher Assessor Training — CAVA advertised around **£369**  
- Carlton Training — CAVA ~**£395 + VAT**; CAVA+IQA bundle ~**£890 + VAT**

### Before you pay, check

- Is it **Ofqual-regulated** (RQF) with a named awarding body?  
- Are **registration + certification** included?  
- Does CAVA require you to assess **real learners** (it usually does)?  
- Can the IQA course be completed with access to assessment decisions to sample?  
- How long is support (often 6–12 months)?

### Cheapest smart path for Muchuo’s

1. You (or lead trainer): **AET (if needed) + CAVA**  
2. Freelance colleague: **IQA Level 4 Award**  
3. Keep CPD logs (short monthly notes + any webinars/reading)

Do **not** buy the cheapest course if it cannot place you with learners to assess — CAVA fails without that practical evidence.

## 4. If I get approved for one AO course, how does *my* course get included?

**It does not automatically.**

Centre approval for “one course” only authorises you to deliver **that listed qualification** (e.g. a specific RQF award the AO already owns).

Your Complete Software Testing Programme is separate. There are two different inclusion methods:

### Method A — Regulated route (AO’s course)

1. Choose an existing Ofqual-regulated qualification from the AO catalogue.  
2. Get centre approval **for that QAN / qualification**.  
3. Deliver **their** syllabus and assessment rules.  
4. Your materials can support teaching, but the certificate learners get is for **the AO’s regulated qualification**, not “Muchuo’s Software Testing” as a national title.

Use this when you want funding / LRS / nationally recognised outcomes.

### Method B — Endorsement / FQP route (your course)

1. Become an approved centre (systems + staff + policies).  
2. Submit **your** programme for endorsement (Focus Awards: FQP / bespoke qualification development request).  
3. Provide learning outcomes, assessment criteria, delivery method, progression, assessment plan.  
4. AO validates quality; you keep IP.  
5. Learners get a **Certificate of Achievement** naming your subject — **not** an RQF certificate.

Use this when you want your existing software-testing course branded/endorsed quickly.

### Visual

```text
AO centre approval
        │
        ├── Approve regulated Qualification X  →  deliver X as written
        │
        └── Endorse YOUR programme (FQP)       →  your course + Certificate of Achievement
```

You can do **both**: e.g. FQP for the commercial testing programme, plus one regulated digital/employability/assessor qual for funding/LRS positioning.

## 5. Direct answers

**“We definitely need Tutor/Assessor/IQA packs first?”**  
Yes.

**“Can one person be all that?”**  
Tutor+Assessor: yes. All three on the same assessments: no — get a separate IQA (freelance is fine).

**“Where cheap online?”**  
Search Ofqual-regulated AET / CAVA / L4 IQA; budget roughly £250–£500 per role, or ~£1k for CAVA+IQA. Verify practical assessment requirements.

**“How does my course become included?”**  
Either map teaching support under a chosen regulated AO qualification, **or** submit your programme separately for FQP/endorsement. Approving one unrelated regulated course does not absorb yours.
