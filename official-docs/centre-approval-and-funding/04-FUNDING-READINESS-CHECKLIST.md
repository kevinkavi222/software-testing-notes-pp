# Funding readiness checklist — local council / government routes

## Goal

Position Muchuo’s Centre to win **subcontract** or **framework** places for Adult Skills Fund / local authority adult learning / Skills Bootcamps.

## Must-have before approaching funders

- [ ] UKPRN active (10102202)
- [ ] ICO registration
- [ ] Insurance (PL + PI)
- [ ] Latest accounts / financial evidence
- [ ] Full policy set (especially Safeguarding, Prevent, EDI, GDPR)
- [ ] Named Tutor, Assessor, IQA (or countersigning arrangements)
- [ ] Awarding-body centre approval for the **funded regulated aims** you will deliver
- [ ] Delivery track record pack (even private cohorts: enrolments, completion, destinations)
- [ ] Ofsted EIF self-assessment / QIP draft

## Where to look

- [ ] Find a Tender alerts for “Adult Skills Fund”, “adult learning”, “Skills Bootcamp”, “digital”
- [ ] Your local council adult learning / employment & skills team
- [ ] Local college partnership / subcontracting pages
- [ ] Combined / mayoral authority skills procurement portal (if in a devolved area)

## Pitch angle for Muchuo’s

Digital / software-testing employability for adults, with:

- structured programme documentation already built  
- pathway into junior QA roles  
- optional regulated digital/IT or employability qualification alongside commercial testing training  

## Do not claim

- That FQP endorsement alone makes the course government-fundable  
- That LRS access already exists  
- Ofqual status for non-regulated products
