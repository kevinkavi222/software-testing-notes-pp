**Organisation:** MUCHUO’S CENTRE FOR TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT LTD  
**UKPRN:** 10102202  

This guide answers three linked questions:

1. What do you need to become an **approved centre** with an awarding body (documents + fees)?  
2. What is Focus Awards **FQP**, and how does it help (or not help)?  
3. What do you need for **local council / government funding**?

---

## 1. First: separate three different “approvals”

| Track | What it is | What it gives you | What it does **not** give you |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. AO centre approval (regulated / RQF)** | Awarding body approves you to deliver their **Ofqual-regulated** qualifications | Nationally recognised certificates; possible PLR/LRS support; stronger funding eligibility | Automatic money from government |
| **B. Endorsement / FQP / customised programmes** | Awarding body quality-stamps **your own** bespoke or CPD course | Credibility + branded Certificate of Achievement | RQF credits, national recognition, PLR, typical ASF funding |
| **C. Public funding (council / DfE / MCA)** | You win a grant, contract, or subcontract to deliver funded learning | Fees paid for eligible learners | Does not replace AO approval if the funded aims are regulated quals |

You can pursue A and B with the same awarding body.  
Funding (C) usually expects A (regulated aims) **plus** quality, finance, and procurement readiness.

## 2. Focus Awards FQP — what it is and how it helps you

**FQP = Focus on Quality Provision** (Focus Awards’ endorsement scheme).

### What FQP does

- Lets an **already approved** Focus Awards centre get **your own** software-testing / CPD programme endorsed.
- You keep ownership of the content (your Student/Instructor guides can sit underneath it).
- Learners get a **Focus Awards Certificate of Achievement**.
- Focus Awards checks your management systems, IQA, and staff so the product looks quality-assured to the market.

### What FQP does **not** do

- It is **not nationally recognised**.
- It does **not** put credits on the RQF.
- It should **not** be assumed to appear on the Personal Learning Record (PLR).
- It will **not**, by itself, fix the LRS rejection.
- It will **not**, by itself, unlock Adult Skills Fund / council funded places (funders almost always want **regulated** learning aims, or separately defined non-regulated community learning under their rules).

### Bottom line for Muchuo’s

Use FQP if you want a **fast commercial credibility stamp** on your Complete Software Testing Programme while you build the regulated/funding path.  
Do **not** treat FQP as the funding or LRS solution.

Official Focus Awards wording: FQP qualifications are **not nationally recognised** and do **not** accrue RQF credits.  
Sources: [FQP page](https://focusawards.org.uk/fqp/), [Non-regulated qualifications](https://focusawards.org.uk/non-regulated-qualifications/), Becoming a Centre Handbook (Nov 2025).

## 3. Becoming a Focus Awards approved centre — documents & fees

Focus Awards is an **Ofqual-regulated awarding organisation**. Centre approval is the door to:

- their **regulated (RQF)** qualifications, and/or  
- **FQP** endorsement of your bespoke courses.

### Documents required (from Focus Awards handbook)

**Application pack**

- [ ] Completed centre application form (send to `business@focusawards.org.uk` or apply online)
- [ ] Staff certificates
- [ ] Copies of staff ID
- [ ] CPD logs
- [ ] Staff CVs
- [ ] Centre policies and procedures
- [ ] Optional but helpful: EQA report from another AO in the last 12 months

**Staff evidence (Tutor / Assessor / IQA — freelance allowed)**

For each role:

- [ ] Certificates  
- [ ] CV  
- [ ] CPD log  
- [ ] Proof of ID  

**Typical staff qualification expectations**

| Role | Expected competence |
|---|---|
| Tutor | Teaching qual (L3/L4/L5 Education & Training or Cert Ed, or equivalent considered) + occupational competence |
| Assessor | Assessing qual (e.g. L3 Award Assessing Competence / Vocationally-Related Achievement, or CAVA) — or trainee with countersigning |
| IQA | IQA qual (e.g. L4 Award Internal Quality Assurance) — or trainee with countersigning |

**Policies Focus Awards asks for**

- Equality and Diversity  
- Malpractice and Maladministration  
- Learner Appeals  
- Complaints  
- Staff Induction and CPD  
- Learner registration and certification (including ID checks)  
- Learner Induction  
- Conflict of interest  
- Internal Quality Assurance  
- Third-party agreements (if any)  
- Data protection / security & confidentiality  
- Plagiarism  
- Whistleblowing  
- Sanctions  
- Safeguarding  

They can send policy exemplars; customise them for Muchuo’s — do not paste blindly.

### Important product-fit note for Muchuo’s

Focus Awards’ regulated catalogue is strongest in areas like sport, beauty, health, teaching/assessor quals — **not** software testing as a primary RQF offer. So for your Centre:

| Goal | Focus Awards fit |
|---|---|
| Endorse your own software testing programme quickly | **Strong** via FQP |
| Deliver Ofqual assessor / education & training quals (build staff capacity) | **Good** |
| Deliver Ofqual IT/software-testing quals for funding/LRS | **Weak / check catalogue** — may need BCS, Pearson, NCFE, City & Guilds, etc. in parallel |

## 4. Other awarding-body routes (fees snapshot)

Use this only as orientation; always confirm current fees with the AO.

| Awarding body | Typical new-centre cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Focus Awards** | £260 + VAT (~£312) | Fast; FQP available; check IT product fit |
| **NCFE** | New application fee **£150** (2026–27 guide) + annual recognition fee (other centres listed around **£1,190** in 2026–27 guide) | Also offers customised/endorsed programmes (separate fees) |
| **Pearson (vocational)** | One-off **£1,000** on successful UK WBL/college approval; **£5,000** annual minimum spend | Heavier process; BTEC/digital suite possible |
| **BCS** | Fees via account manager / product area | Best IT/professional + some Ofqual digital products; ISTQB is separate professional accreditation |

**Endorsement vs full regulated approval — wording cheat-sheet**

- **Endorsement / FQP / customised / endorsed programme** = quality badge on *your* course (usually non-regulated).  
- **Centre approval for RQF / Ofqual quals** = you deliver *their* nationally regulated qualifications.  
- BCS uses **approval** for Ofqual products and **accreditation** for professional certifications (ISTQB). Different words, same idea: regulated vs non-regulated tracks.

## 5. Local council & government funding — what you need

### How funding usually works for a private centre

You rarely “apply to LRS and money appears.” Typical paths:

1. **Subcontract** under a college, local authority adult learning service, or combined authority provider.  
2. **Win a place** on a local/MCA Adult Skills Fund (ASF) framework / tender (Find a Tender + their e-procurement portal).  
3. **Skills Bootcamps / UKSPF / DWP programmes** via regional procurement (digital bootcamps can fit a testing/IT offer).  
4. Much later: direct contract holder status (harder without delivery track record).

Local authorities and colleges keep quality and audit responsibility, so their due diligence is strict.

### Core documents funders usually ask for

Build this pack now — it overlaps heavily with AO centre approval:

**Legal & finance**

- [ ] Companies House active status  
- [ ] UKPRN (you have **10102202**)  
- [ ] ICO registration  
- [ ] Latest accounts / management accounts / bank statements  
- [ ] Public liability + professional indemnity insurance  
- [ ] Directors / ownership details  
- [ ] VAT status  

**Quality & compliance**

- [ ] Full policy set (safeguarding, Prevent, EDI, H&S, complaints, appeals, GDPR, malpractice)  
- [ ] Self-assessment report / quality improvement plan aligned to Ofsted EIF  
- [ ] Tutor/assessor/IQA CVs and certificates  
- [ ] DBS arrangements for staff  
- [ ] Learner support / IAG process  
- [ ] Previous delivery outcomes (retention, achievement, destinations) — even private cohorts help  

**Curriculum & funding fit**

- [ ] AO centre approval letter for the **fundable regulated aims** you will deliver  
- [ ] Mapping of offer to local skills priorities (digital / employability)  
- [ ] Scheme of work, assessment plan, IQA sampling plan  
- [ ] Ability to return ILR / funding data accurately (or work under lead provider systems)

**Procurement readiness**

- [ ] Registered on relevant portals (e.g. Find a Tender alerts; local Chest / Delta / ProContract)  
- [ ] Standard Selection Questionnaire answers ready  
- [ ] References / trade references  

### Reality check

Many tenders prefer providers with:

- prior subcontract delivery, and/or  
- Ofsted Good/Outstanding or a positive monitoring visit  

If you have no Ofsted history yet, prepare a written explanation and show EIF-aligned quality systems. Some frameworks accept that for new providers; many prefer proven delivery.

### Practical funding sequence for Muchuo’s

1. Finish policies + Tutor/Assessor/IQA staffing.  
2. Gain **regulated AO centre approval** for fundable digital/IT (or employability) qualifications that match local priorities.  
3. Optionally add **Focus Awards FQP** to endorse the commercial software-testing programme.  
4. Approach local adult learning / college partnership teams with a one-page offer.  
5. Bid when ASF / bootcamp tenders open in your area.  
6. Once funded (or AO-regulated with PLR upload), reapply to **LRS**.

### Short answers to your questions

**“What do I need to be approved as a centre?”**  
Application form, policies listed above, Tutor/Assessor/IQA evidence (certs, CVs, CPD, ID), and the AO fee. For Focus Awards UK: about **£260 + VAT**, ~7 working days if complete.

**“Documents required / fees included?”**  
See sections 3–4. Focus Awards FQP learner fee is **£13.20** once you are an approved centre.

**“How will Focus Awards FQP help me?”**  
It endorses your own course and gives learners a Focus Awards Certificate of Achievement. It helps marketing and quality signalling. It does **not** make the course Ofqual-regulated, does **not** replace funding contracts, and does **not** by itself satisfy LRS.
