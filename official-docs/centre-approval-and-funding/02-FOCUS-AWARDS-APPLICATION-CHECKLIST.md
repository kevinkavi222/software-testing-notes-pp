## B. Fees to budget (ex VAT unless stated)

- [ ] UK centre approval £260 (+ VAT ≈ £312 total if VAT applies)
- [ ] Year-1 registrations / FQP (£13.20 per FQP learner) or regulated PAYG fees

- [ ] CPD log (last 12 months)
