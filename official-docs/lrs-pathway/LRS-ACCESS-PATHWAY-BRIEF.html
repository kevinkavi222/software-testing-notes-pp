Muchuo’s Centre for Training & Development Ltd

LRS Access Pathway

UKPRN 10102202 · Application AQEC15U7 rejected · Internal decision brief
Why it was rejected: LRS judged the Centre does not currently support government-funded delivery or PLR development. For non-funded providers, both Ofqual awarding-body centre approval and PLR upload of that training by the awarding body are required. An ISTQB-centred private programme does not meet that test on its own.

Eligibility gap

RequirementStatusNotes
UKPRN / UKRLP registration Have 10102202
Programme materials & delivery model Have Student + Instructor guides, course slides, labs
Government funding contract No Not currently relied upon
Ofqual AO centre approval Missing Must obtain before reapplying
Achievements added to PLR by AO Missing Requires Ofqual-regulated qualifications, not ISTQB alone
Recommended route: Become an approved centre with an Ofqual-regulated awarding organisation (best fit for this Centre: BCS) for regulated digital/IT qualifications that can appear on the PLR. Keep ISTQB as a separate professional track if desired. Only then submit a new LRS application with the AO approval evidence.

What not to do

  • Do not reapply to LRS with the same evidence set.
  • Do not treat ISTQB accreditation as equivalent to Ofqual-regulated centre approval.
  • Do not market the Centre as LRS-approved until written approval is received.

Ordered next steps

  1. Send the BCS centre-approval enquiry (regulated products + optional ISTQB).
  2. Optionally confirm evidence expectations with LRS Support.
  3. Complete policies, staff roles, and the BCS business case using the evidence pack checklist.
  4. Obtain written BCS approval for Ofqual-regulated qualifications.
  5. Submit a new LRS application with AO approval + PLR-upload evidence.
