## 2. Why we want to partner with BCS

We want BCS as our awarding / certification partner because:

1. BCS is an Ofqual-recognised awarding organisation for regulated digital/IT qualifications.  
2. Centre approval for regulated products will enable legitimate ULN/PLR support via LRS.  
3. Optional ISTQB accreditation aligns with our existing Complete Software Testing Programme and employer demand.  
4. BCS quality standards match our intent to operate as a credible UK training provider.

## 3. Products requested

### A. Ofqual-regulated (required for LRS pathway)

Request BCS guidance on best-fit products for an adult private IT centre. Initial preference to discuss:

- Essential Digital Skills / digital literacy regulated products (where learner profile fits), and/or  
- Other current BCS regulated digital qualifications suitable for our cohort, and/or  
- Longer-term Digital Modular Programme / HTQ if capacity and demand support it  

State clearly: **we require products whose achievements can be recorded on the Personal Learning Record.**

### B. Professional Certifications (optional commercial track)

- ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (and related foundation extensions later if demand exists)

State clearly: we understand ISTQB is **not** Ofqual-regulated and is separate from the regulated approval needed for LRS.

## 7. Commercial model

- Fee-paying private provision initially  
- Explore funded routes later only if compliant and capacity allows  
- Exam fees / registration fees passed through per BCS pricing  
- No misrepresentation of BCS, Ofqual, or LRS status in marketing

## 8. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Confusing ISTQB with Ofqual quals in marketing | Separate product pages; clear “not Ofqual-regulated” wording for ISTQB |
| Low year-1 regulated volumes | Start with one regulated product family; conservative forecasts |
| Staffing for invigilation | Nominate and train at least two invigilators before go-live |
| LRS reapplication delay | Only apply to LRS after written BCS regulated centre approval |

1. Confirm recommended regulated product family for our profile.  
2. Confirm PLR upload / ULN validation process for those products.  
3. Provide application fee schedule, contract, and timeline.  
4. Open centre approval (regulated) and, if appropriate, ISTQB accreditation in parallel.
