## 1. What the rejection actually means

LRS access is **not** granted because an organisation trains people, has a UKPRN, or has strong course materials.

DfE will only approve access where the organisation supports at least one of these goals:

1. **Government-funded** education/training access, **or**
2. Development of the **Personal Learning Record (PLR)**

For a **non-funded** provider, both of the following must be true:

| Requirement | What it means | Current position |
|---|---|---|
| A. Accredited/approved by an **Ofqual-regulated awarding body** | You are an approved centre for regulated qualifications | **Not met** (no AO centre approval evidenced) |
| B. That awarding body **adds your learners’ achievements to the PLR** | The qualification is Ofqual-regulated and uploaded by the AO | **Not met** for an ISTQB-only pathway |

Reapplying with the same evidence will be rejected again.

## 2. How this relates to what we have already built

Existing materials (Student Programme Guide, Instructor Guide, slide decks) position the Centre as a **job-ready software testing provider** with a strong **ISTQB Foundation** strand.

That work is valuable for delivery and marketing. It does **not**, by itself, satisfy LRS.

Critical distinction (BCS / Ofqual):

- **ISTQB (via BCS or others)** = professional certification → usually needs **accreditation**, and is **explicitly not Ofqual-regulated**
- **Ofqual-regulated BCS qualifications** (e.g. Essential Digital Skills, ICDL/digital literacy, Digital Modular Programme / HTQ, some Level 3–4 IT) = need **centre approval**, and these are the ones that can support **PLR** / LRS eligibility

Official BCS wording on ISTQB Foundation: *“This qualification is not regulated by … Ofqual …”*  
GOV.UK PLR guidance: the PLR *“only shows qualifications that are regulated by Ofqual”* (and unfunded quals may still not appear).

So: **ISTQB alone cannot unlock LRS.**

## 3. Viable routes (ranked for this Centre)

### Route 1 — Recommended: Become an Ofqual AO approved centre (then reapply to LRS)

**Best fit AO for this Centre’s IT/testing focus: BCS (The Chartered Institute for IT).**

Do this in two layers:

1. **Must-have for LRS:** BCS **approval** to deliver at least one **Ofqual-regulated** product family (examples to discuss with BCS):
   - Essential Digital Skills (EDSQ) — Entry 3 / Level 1  
   - Other digital literacy / ICDL-style regulated products  
   - Digital Modular Programme (Level 4) / HTQ where feasible longer-term  
2. **Optional parallel (career product):** BCS **accreditation** for ISTQB / Professional Certifications — keeps the current programme commercially strong, but **does not replace** regulated approval for LRS.

**After AO approval is in writing**, reapply to LRS with:

- AO centre approval / accreditation letter naming the Ofqual-regulated qualifications  
- Confirmation that achievements are (or will be) uploaded to learners’ PLRs by that AO  
- UKPRN, ICO number, Companies House director signatory, super-user details  
- Clear business need: register own learners for ULNs so regulated achievements can be validated/uploaded

### Route 2 — Partner delivery under an already-approved provider

Deliver the testing programme as a satellite / subcontract / affiliate under:

- an FE college, or  
- an established private provider that already holds AO centre approval + LRS access  

They own the regulated registrations / ULNs; Muchuo’s delivers teaching under their quality framework. Faster operationally; less independence.

### Route 3 — Government funding contract

Secure a public funding relationship (e.g. adult skills / AEB subcontract, bootcamp, apprenticeship delivery via an approved lead). Funding alone can justify LRS, but for a small independent centre this is usually slower and more compliance-heavy than Route 1.

### Route 4 — Proceed without LRS (only if goal is purely commercial ISTQB/job-ready training)

If the only goal is private fee-paying learners and ISTQB exam readiness, **LRS is not required**. Continue with current materials and seek BCS ISTQB accreditation for brand/exam delivery quality.  
Choose this only if you do **not** need ULNs, PLR contribution, or a funded/regulated pathway.
