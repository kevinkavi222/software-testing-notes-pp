# Evidence Pack Checklist — AO Centre Approval & Future LRS Reapplication

Use this pack twice:

1. **Now** — to support BCS (or other AO) centre approval  
2. **Later** — to support a new LRS application after AO approval is granted

Tick items as they are collected. Store copies in a single secure folder named  
`MUCHUO-LRS-AO-Evidence-Pack`.

---

## A. Legal & identity (organisation)

- [ ] Companies House certificate / incorporation extract (active company)
- [ ] Full legal name matches LRS/UKRLP exactly: **MUCHUO’S CENTRE FOR TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT LTD**
- [ ] UKPRN confirmation screenshot/PDF from UKRLP: **10102202**
- [ ] ICO registration number + register screenshot (mandatory for LRS)
- [ ] Registered office + delivery/training addresses
- [ ] Website URL showing training offer
- [ ] Director details (must match Companies House for LRS agreement signature)

---

## B. People & roles

- [ ] Named **Centre Manager / Super User** (for AO systems and later LRS)
- [ ] Named authorised signatory (director)
- [ ] Tutor CVs / CPD records for software testing & digital delivery
- [ ] Invigilator(s) identified (required for many BCS assessments)
- [ ] Safeguarding / Prevent lead named
- [ ] Data Protection Officer / privacy contact named

---

## C. Quality & policies (AO will expect these)

- [ ] Quality assurance policy
- [ ] Equality, diversity & inclusion / equal opportunities policy
- [ ] Appeals & complaints policy
- [ ] Access arrangements / reasonable adjustments policy
- [ ] Malpractice & maladministration policy
- [ ] Data protection / GDPR & learner privacy notice (LRS privacy notice text ready)
- [ ] Health & safety (if in-person sites)
- [ ] Safeguarding & Prevent policy
- [ ] Assessment security / invigilation procedures
- [ ] Internal verification / internal quality assurance process

If any of these do not yet exist, draft them **before** the BCS application — AOs commonly raise pre-approval actions on missing policies.

---

## D. Programme evidence already produced (reuse)

These show delivery seriousness; they do **not** replace AO approval.

- [ ] Student Programme Guide (HTML/PDF)
- [ ] Instructor Guide (HTML/PDF)
- [ ] Complete Software Testing Course slides + presenters guide
- [ ] Sample scheme of work / 12–16 week timetable
- [ ] Assessment/mock approach (ISTQB Fridays, portfolio checklist)
- [ ] Learner support model (tutor hours, weekend live sessions)

---

## E. Awarding organisation evidence (the missing piece)

- [ ] Written enquiry sent to BCS (or other AO)
- [ ] Business case accepted by AO account manager
- [ ] Signed AO centre contract
- [ ] Application fee paid (retain invoice/receipt)
- [ ] Centre approval / accreditation letter or certificate
- [ ] List of **Ofqual-regulated** qualification numbers (QANs) approved for the centre
- [ ] Written confirmation that the AO uploads achievements to the **PLR** for those quals
- [ ] (Optional) Separate ISTQB / Professional Certifications accreditation — commercial track only

---

## F. LRS-specific items (only after E is complete)

- [ ] Proof of AO accreditation/approval (PDF)
- [ ] Funding contract **or** clear statement that provision is non-funded but AO-accredited with PLR upload
- [ ] Estimated annual learner volumes
- [ ] Business need statement: “register our own learners for ULNs to support AO achievement upload to PLR”
- [ ] Super-user contact details
- [ ] Signed LRS Organisation Agreement by a Companies House director
- [ ] New LRS application submitted (new reference number retained)

---

## G. Evidence that will **not** satisfy LRS on its own

Do not rely on these as the primary eligibility proof:

- ISTQB syllabus alignment alone  
- Course slides / programme guides alone  
- UKPRN alone  
- Companies House registration alone  
- Website screenshots alone  
- Letters of intent without AO centre approval
